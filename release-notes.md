# 🚀 AIRI v0.9.44-stable.20261111 — Release Notes

This release delivers a massive leap across companion ubiquity, local neural intelligence, dynamic 3D dance choreography, and developer ergonomics: introducing the **Persistent Desktop System Tray Companion**—giving you instant hardware diagnostics, runtime telemetry, voice dictation, and companion access right from your macOS and Windows menu bar—alongside the **Needle 3 Subconscious Daydreaming & Pacing Engine** with dual-channel reasoning extraction, **Cactus Whistle 100% Local Speech-to-Text**, **Motion Choreography Studio** with dynamic musical phrasing and anatomical safety clamping, **Cloudflare Workers AI Artistry**, the **Code Assistant Notifications Studio**, **IDOL 3D Gaussian Splats**, and **Kokoro-82M Voice Cloning**.

Companions can now live seamlessly in your desktop menu bar without cluttering your workspace, daydream and pace their thoughts organically during conversation, transcribe speech entirely on-device without cloud latency, choreograph fluid rhythmic dances synchronized to music beats with zero backwards neck hyperextension, paint art using Cloudflare's serverless AI catalog, and connect directly to your development workflows and coding agents.

---

## ⚡ Staying Current: In-App Over-The-Air (OTA) Updates

As a quick reminder, you do not need to manually download massive installer packages on GitHub for routine updates!

Heading to **Settings > About** inside AIRI is the fastest and easiest way to stay up to date. Delta updates download seamlessly in the background (typically just 5–15 MB) and apply in seconds across your selected channel (**Stable**, **Canary**, **Dev**, or **Nightly**). Full GitHub installer downloads remain reserved for new setups and major platform checkpoints.

## ✨ Key Highlights

### 🖥️ Desktop System Tray Companion & Live Status Dashboard
- **Persistent Menu Bar Companion**: Access AIRI anytime directly from the macOS menu bar or Windows system tray without keeping the primary stage window open.
- **Compact Quick-Chat & Whisper Dictation**: Talk or dictate messages using local Whisper transcription directly inside a sleek, theme-aware popover companion card with dynamic chromatic accents.
- **Live Hardware & Runtime Telemetry**: Monitor real-time CPU utilization, system RAM, active VRAM footprint, LLM token generation velocities, and speech pipeline latencies directly on the companion dashboard.
- **Quick Action Presets & Multi-Surface Routing**: One-click controls to toggle stage visibility, mute audio synthesis, adjust companion presence, and route directly to customizer tools.
- **Prewarmed Window Boot**: The tray companion prewarms silently in the background for zero-latency, instantaneous popover reveals on click.

### 🧠 Needle 3: Subconscious Daydreaming, Dual-Channel Reasoning & Turn Pacing
- **Subconscious Daydreaming (Toggle 4)**: Companions now feature autonomous internal cadence and daydream beat extraction, contemplating thoughts and forming deeper thematic associations between turns.
- **Dual-Channel Reasoning Extraction**: Transparently separates internal chain-of-thought (CoT) deliberation from user-facing dialogue across thinking models, preventing reasoning traces from leaking into speech synthesis.
- **Anti-Echo Prompt Framing**: Eliminates conversational echoes and redundant parrot-backs, ensuring companions respond with fresh perspective and natural initiative.
- **Pragmatic WASM Probe & Opportunistic Prewarming**: Evaluates conversational intents and prewarms upcoming turn contexts in a lightweight WebAssembly thread, lowering perceived interaction latency.
- **Dynamic Aside Audio Caching**: Caches conversational asides and thinking fillers on the fly for fluid, uninterrupted turn delivery.

### 🎙️ Cactus Whistle Local STT & Cactus Mirage Vision
- **100% On-Device Whistle Speech Recognition**: Integrated Cactus Whistle running locally via WebAssembly and a dedicated audio resampler, delivering private, zero-latency speech transcription without external cloud APIs.
- **Default Onboarding STT Hero**: Whistle Local is now elevated as a primary, zero-setup speech recognition choice during first-run Onboarding V3.
- **Cactus Mirage Local Vision Coprocessor**: Lightweight local vision embeddings and perceptual salience evaluation for desktop screen monitoring.
- **Spatial Bounding & Foveal Zoom Lab**: Added developer inspection tooling to visualize screen salience bounding boxes and focus regions.

### 💃 Motion Choreography: Dynamic Musical Phrasing & Kinematic Safety
- **Dynamic Musical Phrasing Cycle Engine**: Companions can now choreograph multi-measure dance routines that dynamically synchronize to musical downbeats, tempo shifts, and acoustic phrasing layers.
- **Hermite Seam Smoothing**: Eliminates motion jarring and seam snaps during continuous FlowMDM loop cycles, ensuring silky transitions between dance motifs.
- **Cervical Kinematic Safety Clamping**: Anatomically clamps neck pitch to physiologically safe limits, permanently preventing unnatural backward neck hyperextension during dynamic head movement.
- **Continuous Acoustic Layering & Auto-Dance**: Automatically triggers rhythmic swaying and motion cycles in response to playing audio tracks with zero manual configuration.

### 🎨 Autonomous Artistry: Cloudflare Workers AI & Provider Expansion
- **Cloudflare Workers AI Provider**: Integrated Cloudflare's serverless Workers AI image generation suite, unlocking a 9-model catalog (including Flux, SDXL, and specialized anime checkpoints) accessible via personal Cloudflare accounts.
- **Wiro AI Provider Integration**: Added first-class support for Wiro AI models across companion chat and generation tasks.
- **Provider Catalog Baseline Refresh**: Synchronized the free AI model catalog baseline to November 2026 across 90 connected platforms.
- **Artistry Status Badges**: Added unified connection badges and health indicators across Cloudflare, OpenAI, and local ComfyUI image backends in Onboarding V3.

### 💻 Code Assistant Notifications Studio & Persistent CLI Bridge
- **Local Notification Studio**: Dedicated settings dashboard and preview stage for routing notifications, terminal outputs, and build events from coding assistants into AIRI.
- **H3 REST Endpoints & Persistent WebSocket Listener**: Local HTTP and WebSocket server infrastructure allowing external IDE tools, CLI agents, and background scripts to deliver alerts with authentication headers and CORS preflight support.
- **airi-assistant CLI**: Lightweight command-line utility and agent quick-connectors to pipe build statuses, test failures, or task completions directly to your companion.

### ✨ IDOL 3D Gaussian Splats & 3D Avatar Rendering
- **3D Gaussian Splatting Viewer**: Integrated `@mkkellogg/gaussian-splats-3d` directly into the Three.js rendering stage, unlocking volumetric, photorealistic 3D environment and character splat playback.
- **IDOL Architecture RFC**: Established architectural foundation for Gaussian splat avatar rendering, lighting integration, and memory-efficient splat streaming.

### 🗣️ Kokoro-82M Custom Voice Cloning & Speech Polish
- **Acoustic Manifold Blending**: Create bespoke voice personalities in Kokoro-82M by blending acoustic feature weights across multiple voice references without GPU retraining.
- **Native OpenRouter Speech Discovery**: Dynamic discovery and synthesis support for native speech models hosted on OpenRouter.
- **Natural URL & Obsidian Link Pronunciation**: Companions now pronounce web addresses and Markdown/Obsidian links naturally as spoken words instead of spelling out raw slashes and punctuation.
- **Punctuation Lookahead & CJK Emphasis**: Preserved punctuation lookahead buffers and fixed bold/italic Markdown emphasis rendering adjacent to full-width CJK punctuation marks.

### 🎭 Live2D Touch Reactions, Focus Bias & MAGIC Crossfading
- **Continuous Head-Pat & Tap Reactions**: Live2D avatars now dynamically track cursor strokes and respond with contextual expressions and animations when patted or clicked.
- **MAGIC Motion Focus Bias**: Avatars smoothly turn their gaze and upper-body posture toward the mouse cursor during idle states.
- **Speech Profile Crossfading**: Seamlessly crossfades avatar motion profiles into expressive speaking states the instant audio synthesis begins.
- **Mobile Stage Live2D Editor**: Added a stage-first Live2D model editor optimized for mobile and pocket viewports with animated height transitions.

### 🛠️ Platform Stability & Ergosphere Delivery Hardening
- **Stage-Mate Auto-Fetch**: The desktop app now automatically resolves repository paths and fetches prebuilt Stage-Mate Unity sidecar binaries when launching VRM/Unity avatar stages.
- **Linux WebGL & Click-Through Fix**: Resolved transparent window click-through issues on Linux desktop environments while preserving hardware-accelerated WebGL rendering.
- **Rollup Treeshaking & Canonicalize Bundling**: Optimized production bundle treeshaking and bundled RFC 8785 canonicalization for reliable cryptographic OTA manifest processing.
- **Automated Desktop Snapshot Backups**: Added user-configurable controls in Data Settings to manage automatic desktop workspace state snapshots.
